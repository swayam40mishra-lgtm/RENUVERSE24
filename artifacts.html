ARTIFACT SUMMARY

The first physical artifact recovered by Project Aurora.

Delivered on 19 September 2026, the Black Box marks the beginning of Aurora Week and the first confirmed transfer between the archive and its recipient.

What appeared externally as a mysterious black archive container contained something unexpected: a signal from another world.

The artifact was constructed around a Harry Potter / Hogwarts-inspired theme, transforming the first Aurora delivery into a miniature wizarding archive.

It was not designed merely to be opened. It was designed to establish the first contact.

ARCHIVE PURPOSE

WHY THE BLACK
BOX EXISTS

01

FIRST CONTACT

The Black Box is the physical beginning of Project Aurora.

Before the Headquarters existed, before the archive could be explored digitally, Aurora needed something tangible.

Something that could arrive without explanation. Something that could simply say:

The first signal has arrived.
02

THE FIRST RECOVERED RECORD

Artifact 001 establishes the archive's basic principle:

Aurora does not simply give things. Aurora records them.

The Black Box therefore becomes the first object entered into the Artifact Archive.

03

THE FIRST UNEXPECTED WORLD

The Harry Potter theme introduces the idea that Aurora may occasionally intersect with other fictional or cultural worlds.

Within the Aurora system, this is treated as an associated signal, not as the identity of Aurora itself.

Aurora remains the primary archive. The wizarding theme is the first anomaly.
