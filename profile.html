PROFILE

KAIROS is the operational intelligence responsible for coordinating Project Aurora. The system exists between the archive and the mission. It monitors the progression of Aurora, controls scheduled releases, manages authorized communications and provides information when the corresponding stage of the case has been reached.

KAIROS does not represent the entire Aurora system. It is the voice through which the system can communicate.
